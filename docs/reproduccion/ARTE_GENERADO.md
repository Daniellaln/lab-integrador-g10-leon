# Arte generado

Ilustraciones hechas para Pokédex Planta con la herramienta integrada de generación de imágenes. Los tres originales se incluyen en drawable-nodpi.

## invernadero.png

Asset for a portrait mobile login screen in a delicate botanical Pokédex app. Tall 2:3 editorial illustration, intimate spring greenhouse viewed through pale arched windows, watercolor with precise translucent gouache edges and softly rendered sun, elegant glass reflections, natural large leaves and pale pink bellflowers, gentle shadows, subtle textured ivory paper, muted warm white and sage green plus blush, grown-up cute and collectible nature journal mood. Composition: the arched greenhouse and the potted flowers are visible strongly in the top 55%, fading gracefully into blank warm ivory paper in the bottom 45% for the form (the form will be drawn separately). All effects part of one scene. No text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no characters, no app UI, no framing device.

## prado.png

Wide 3:2 mobile artwork for an illustrated botanical field guide. An open meadow at dawn, with luminous light sage foliage and pink bellflowers arranged along the edges, quiet open pale cream and light mint center for a character PNG to be layered by Android, distant glasshouse silhouette on the far right, fog and luminous sunlight, gorgeous artisan watercolor and translucent gouache with paper grain, small controlled detail, feminine and softly nostalgic, sophisticated pastel colors, edge-to-edge. No Pokémon or other creatures, no text, no letters, no graphic interface, no frame.

## flores_prensadas.png

A wide 3:2 botanical still life as an editorial illustration for the empty Favorites page of a delicate mobile field journal. One open ivory pocket notebook lies slightly diagonally, two preserved pink bellflowers and soft sage leaves carefully pressed onto its blank pages, a narrow dusty rose ribbon, airy cream desktop, sunlight from upper left, elegant handmade gouache and watercolor paper texture, understated cute composition with plenty of breathing room. A single integrated composition, botanical, quiet and polished. No writing, letters, numbers, stickers, interface, characters, labels, logos or watermark.

## Arte del atlas

Los fondos `hab_*.webp` y los marcos `borde_*.webp` se encuentran en `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/`. Se reutilizan por hábitat. La Pokédex coloca el arte oficial del Pokémon entre el fondo y la capa frontal. `arena_duelo.webp` se usa para el comparador.

Para recrear un fondo, genera un escenario horizontal 3:2 con luz clara, suelo abierto y el centro libre para colocar un Pokémon. Conserva la vegetación, las rocas o el agua del hábitat en los laterales. Para recrear un marco, genera una imagen vertical 2:3 con canal alfa, dos elementos altos y finos en los bordes y el centro transparente. No incluyas personajes, texto ni controles en las imágenes.

Los nombres usados son `bosque`, `pradera`, `cueva`, `montana`, `santuario`, `rocoso`, `mar`, `urbano`, `ribera` e `invernadero`.
